Arabic text-line recognizer used for Arabic-to-English translation (pipeline.ARABIC_REC).

Source:   https://huggingface.co/medyas/arabic_PP-OCRv6_small_rec
Revision: 0f1155db29389258f18e9232a770859719d3c6e9 (files from its inference/ folder)
License:  Apache-2.0
Model:    PP-OCRv6_small_rec fine-tuned on synthetic printed Arabic; outputs text in visual order,
          which pipeline.logical_order() reverses.

Unlike the official PaddleOCR models, PaddleX cannot download this one automatically, so it is kept
here and excluded from the models/ ignore rule. To fetch it again and verify it:

  REV=0f1155db29389258f18e9232a770859719d3c6e9
  for f in inference.json inference.pdiparams inference.yml; do
    curl -fL -o $f https://huggingface.co/medyas/arabic_PP-OCRv6_small_rec/resolve/$REV/inference/$f
  done
  sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
